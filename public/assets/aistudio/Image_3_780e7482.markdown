## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for modern university and early-career students managing recurring costs, shared bills, and tight daily budgets. The interface rejects gamified visual noise, aggressive neons, and gendered cues in favor of a calm, utilitarian, and understated dark architecture.

The personality balances computational rigor with quiet assurance:
- **Tone**: Focused, pragmatic, discreet, and reliable.
- **Atmosphere**: A low-fatigue workspace calibrated for evening study sessions and rapid on-the-go transaction logging.
- **Design Movement**: Systematic Minimalist Modern. Structured information density is paired with disciplined slate-teal tones, crisp hairline borders, and functional data contrast.

## Colors

The palette establishes an analytical, neutral-dark environment using desaturated slate, cool graphite, and muted slate-teal highlights.

### Palette Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#121518`)**: Deep charcoal graphite providing eye relief and infinite ground depth.
- **Surface Elevation (`#1A1E23`, `#22272E`)**: Subtly lifted tonal containers for data groupings, transaction lists, and analytics widgets.
- **Primary Accent (`#4A6B82`)**: A tempered slate-teal used for primary affordances, active filters, and positive financial balance indicators without resorting to garish emeralds.
- **Secondary & Tertiary Elements (`#6C8299`, `#5E7C8D`)**: Controlled cool-slate variations used for category tags, subtle data visualizations, and progress tracks.
- **Text & Foreground**:
  - Primary text: Soft off-white (`#E6EDF3`) achieving AAA contrast without harsh optical flare.
  - Secondary text: Neutral slate gray (`#8B949E`) for metadata, timestamps, and recurring payment labels.
  - Borders & Dividers: Cool slate hairline (`#30363D`).

## Typography

The type scale relies exclusively on Inter to provide typographic neutrality, balanced tabular numeral alignment, and crisp rendering on screens of any density.

### Principles
- **Tabular Figures**: Numeric totals, balances, and transaction rows must leverage OpenType `tnum` features to maintain strict vertical alignments across columns.
- **Dense Hierarchies**: Weight shifts (400 regular, 500 medium, 600 semibold) demarcate financial priority rather than exaggerated size jumps.
- **Tight Kerning**: Headlines apply negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor numeric summaries cleanly within widgets.

## Layout & Spacing

A compact, responsive layout model suited for high-density information. The grid utilizes 4 columns on mobile, 8 columns on tablet, and a 12-column fluid structure on desktop with a capped maximum width of 1280px.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Rhythm Multiplier**: All layout gaps, gutters, and paddings adhere strictly to a 4px/8px base cadence.
- **Mobile Density**: Compact paddings (`space-sm` to `space-md`) prevent vertical scroll bloat, keeping month-to-date spending summaries above the mobile fold.
- **Desktop Distribution**: Metrics, category velocity, and transaction ledgers reflow into a 3-column dashboard arrangement (4-col primary metrics, 5-col transaction table, 3-col recurring commitments).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through **tonal layering and low-contrast perimeter boundaries** rather than ambient drop shadows, maintaining a clean, architectural aesthetic.

### Layer Architecture
- **Base Level (`#121518`)**: Application viewport canvas.
- **Container Level 1 (`#1A1E23`)**: Standard cards, budget overview containers, and side rail navigation. Enclosed by a 1px solid border (`#30363D`).
- **Container Level 2 (`#22272E`)**: Hovered list entries, secondary cards, inner metric pods, and segmented controls.
- **Floating Overlays (`#272D35`)**: Popovers, modal dialogs, and date pickers. Enhanced with a subtle, non-diffuse utility shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` bordered with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework follows level `1` (Soft):
- **Base Surfaces & Inputs**: `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii for a precise, technical silhouette.
- **Cards & Data Modules**: `rounded-lg` at `0.5rem` (8px), softening structural boundaries without feeling playful or juvenile.
- **Modal Dialogs & Floating Sheets**: `rounded-xl` at `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Badges & Progress Trackers**: Maintain the 4px baseline, keeping line edges rectilinear and orderly across multi-row feeds.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#4A6B82`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, 4px border radius. Hover: `#5A7E98`. Focus ring: 2px offset slate outline.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Background `transparent`, text `#E6EDF3`, border `1px solid #30363D`. Hover: `#22272E`.
- **Destructive**: Background `rgba(180, 75, 75, 0.15)`, text `#F87171`, border `1px solid rgba(248, 113, 113, 0.3)`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Numeric Inputs**: Background `#161A1E`, border `1px solid #30363D`, text `#E6EDF3`, placeholder `#6E7681`. Focus state transitions to `1px solid #4A6B82` with no chromatic glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16px square/circle with `1px solid #4A6B82`. Checked state fills `#4A6B82` displaying crisp off-white vector marks.

### Cards & Ledger Lists
- **Cards**: Background `#1A1E23`, border `1px solid #30363D`, padding `space-lg`, border-radius 8px.
- **Transaction Rows**: Flat tabular rows with subtle horizontal hairline borders (`#21262D`). Left-aligned date and merchant description, right-aligned monetary value using tabular numbers. Hover background tint `#1E232A`.

### Category Chips & Badges
- Compact padding (`0.25rem 0.5rem`), font `label-sm`, border-radius 4px.
- Background `rgba(108, 130, 153, 0.12)`, text `#9FB1C1`, border `1px solid rgba(108, 130, 153, 0.25)`.

### Budget Progress Indicators
- **Track**: `#22272E`, height 6px, border-radius 2px.
- **Indicator Fill**: Solid `#4A6B82` for normal spend thresholds; switches to `#8A785D` (muted amber) at 85% limit and `#8C5353` (subdued crimson) only upon breach.
